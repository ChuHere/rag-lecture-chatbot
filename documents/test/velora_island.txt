VELORA ISLAND REFERENCE

Velora is a fictional island nation with three cities: Arin, Belmar, and
Corda. Arin is the capital and has 82,000 residents. Belmar has 47,000
residents and contains Velora's only international airport. Corda has 31,000
residents and is known for producing blue saffron.

Velora's rainy season runs from March through June. Visitors are prohibited
from entering the northern forest during the rainy season because the trails
are unsafe. The annual Lantern Festival takes place in Corda on September 18.

Travel between Arin and Belmar takes 45 minutes by electric train. Travel
between Belmar and Corda takes 70 minutes. There is no direct train between
Arin and Corda. A traveler going from Arin to Corda by train must change trains
in Belmar, making the total scheduled travel time 115 minutes.

The national currency is the vel, abbreviated VEL. The official language is
Veloran, although most airport employees also speak English.
