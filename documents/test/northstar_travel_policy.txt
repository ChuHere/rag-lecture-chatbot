NORTHSTAR COMPANY TRAVEL POLICY

Employees must receive written approval from their department manager before
booking international travel. Domestic trips costing less than 800 dollars do
not require manager approval. Domestic trips costing 800 dollars or more
require manager approval before booking.

The standard hotel limit is 180 dollars per night. In Harbor City, the hotel
limit is 240 dollars per night because of higher accommodation costs. Any hotel
booking above the applicable limit requires approval from the finance
department.

Employees may claim up to 65 dollars per day for meals. Alcoholic drinks are
not reimbursable. Itemized receipts are required for individual expenses of
25 dollars or more.

Expense reports must be submitted within 14 calendar days after the employee
returns. Reports submitted late require a written explanation. Business-class
air travel is permitted only when the scheduled flight duration exceeds eight
hours and a vice president approves the booking.

Employees should use the company booking portal whenever possible. If the
portal is unavailable, they may book directly and include a screenshot of the
portal error with the expense report.
