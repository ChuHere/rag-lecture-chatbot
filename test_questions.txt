Index the two files under documents/test before running these questions.

Labels:
- PRESENT: The answer is explicitly available in the named document.
- ABSENT: The answer is not available and the chatbot should say that it could
  not find relevant information in the indexed lecture documents.

   Expected: Corda; it has 31,000 residents and produces blue saffron.

5. [ABSENT] What is Velora's national animal?
